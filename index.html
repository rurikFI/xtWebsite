The Problem

Coating pipes and cables
shouldn't be a mess

Traditional methods leave uneven coverage, waste material, and take far too long. Getting a uniform coat around a round surface by hand is nearly impossible.

Uneven Coverage

Manual application always misses spots, leaving gaps that compromise insulation and protection.

Time-Consuming

Wrapping and coating by hand takes ages, especially on long pipes or cables.

Material Waste

Over-applying in some spots, missing others — inconsistency wastes expensive materials.

How It Works

4 Simple Steps to
Perfect Coverage

The Xtruder™ makes professional-grade coating accessible to everyone.

1

Select Material

Choose your coating — epoxy, silicone, latex, paint, or any compatible material.

2

Attach Xtruder

Twist the Xtruder™ onto your material tube. It locks in securely, ready for application.

3

Position & Lock

Clamp onto the object you want to coat — pipe, cable, hose, or rope.

4

Apply Evenly

Slide along the surface. The Xtruder™ applies coating uniformly around the full 360° circumference.

360° Uniform Coverage

No gaps. No drips. No waste. Just perfect, professional-grade coating — every time.
